# Figure 5: PITX2c promoter chromatin accessibility retains left/right information through the HH17/16 trough

**PITX2c-promoter chromatin is left-biased in the ventral pool from HH17/16 — where PITX2 RNA no longer reliably resolves the two halves — through HH25, while the PITX2a/b promoters (within-locus controls) and the dorsal compartment show no consistent bias.**

**(A)** The side-defining signals by stage: snRNA PITX2 expression, PITX2c-promoter accessibility, and the PITX2a/b-promoter controls; one point per embryo half (Left = blue circle, Right = red square; bar = median; point size = nuclei), paired halves joined by grey segments. At HH13, HH21 and HH25 the sides come from the snRNA inference (Table S4), independently corroborated by the chromatin separation (paired Wilcoxon p in Table S5); at HH17/16 the side is defined by PITX2c-promoter accessibility itself (Table S5), so no p is reported; embryos 17-5 and 17-6, each a single left half sequenced in two libraries (Figure S7A), are set to left from the design.

**(B)** Left- and right-biased feature counts by stage and compartment (Left up/blue, Right down/red): snRNA differentially expressed genes (Table S6), PITX2-locus peaks in the focal 40-kb window, and peaks in the ±500-kb window (DESeq2, Love et al. 2014; genes embryo-paired, padj < 0.05; peaks fitted over all embryo halves without an embryo term, locus-restricted FDR < 0.1). At HH17/16 the ventral transcriptome shows only two genes, both right-biased (AHR, CHRM2; Table S6), while its PITX2 locus already has eight left-biased peaks (descriptive; Methods); the dorsal control reaches significance at neither chromatin scale, at any stage (underpowered at this promoter); HH13 has no dorsal compartment (blank, not tested); 0 marks a tested side with no biased feature.

**(C)** Left/right chromatin pileup over the focal window (Left up, Right down; lanes depth-normalized to peak-free background L/R = 1), spanning the PITX2a/b/c promoters and a +5.4-kb distal element; GC% track (CpG island at the PITX2c promoter) and gene model below. Stars = significant in the embryo-paired within-population test (locus-restricted FDR; Methods). The HH17/16 lanes (ventral marked †) are not tested because their sides are defined by this signal (A). The HH13 lane is descriptive because of its small sample size. The HH21 and HH25 dorsal lanes are tested but not significant.

**(D)** Among the promoters of the genes that become left/right-biased at HH21, only the PITX2c promoter is already lateralized at the HH17/16 trough (volcano: x = trough left/right effect, y = family-FDR significance, dashed line = FDR 0.1; color = HH21 side; unpaired design).
