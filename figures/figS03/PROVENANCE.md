## Methodology trimmed from the legend

### Panel A
- Sub-panel codes A1–A7. Dodge: dark = all cellranger-arc-called cells (`is_cell==1`); light = downstream snATAC-QC-passed (TSS ≥ 2 & HQ fragments ≥ 1000).
- A6 FRiP is computed at the ArchR 283,818-peak union. A7 AMULET doublet rate at FDR q < 0.01 (12 libraries 3.4–7.8%, median ~5.8%).

### Panel B
- Production ArchR union peak set: 283,818 fixed-width 501-bp peaks. Sub-panel codes B1–B4.
- **B1/B2 are a pool-free yield on the production union.** `figures/data/f2f_peaks_detected_per_population.tsv` counts, per current stage × LPM population, the union peaks detected in that group's nuclei on `step_f2_peak_matrix_archr.h5ad` (249,210 nuclei × 283,818 peaks; 37,472 nuclei carry a current LPM label via the f1 sidecar → SSOT, the g1 recipe). Every threshold is a column: `n_peaks_any` (≥ 1 fragment) and `n_peaks_cpm1` (CPM ≥ 1) saturate at ~the whole union for any group above a few hundred nuclei; `n_peaks_frac01` (≥ 1% of nuclei) collapses onto the 3-nucleus floor for the small groups (n = 42–131) and so mixes thresholds; **`n_peaks_frac05` (≥ 1 fragment in ≥ 5% of nuclei, min 3) is the plotted column** (`DET_COL`) — 14k–37k peaks, comparable across groups, with the small groups (HH13; HH21 Mesothelium n = 55) visibly noisy in B2. Rows = 25 stage × population groups incl. HH13 (which yielded no reproducible ArchR pool). Why not "peaks called per pool": the union was CALLED per ArchR pool (`addReproduciblePeakSet`, MACS3 per pseudobulk replicate, iterative-overlap merge) on an earlier d2g run, those pool labels are retired names, and the pools differ from today's clusters in membership (measured below); re-calling on current clusters would move the union every result is defined on (`archr_build_peaks.R` header: an unseeded rebuild gave 277,604 vs 283,818). `figures/data/archr_pool_composition.tsv` (built from the saved ArchR project) records how the peak-calling pools relate to today's clusters:
  - **The pools were drawn on an earlier, unseeded d2g run; the seeded rerun changed cluster MEMBERSHIP, not just names.** Pure (≥ 95%): `HH17/16|BARX1+` (100% BARX1+), `HH17/16|Cardiopharyngeal LPM` (99%), `HH21|BARX1+` (100%), `HH25|Chondrogenic LPM` (97% today's Syringeal), `HH25|Syringeal` (100% today's Ventral spl. — the retired subcluster), plus the three non-LPM pools (Dermomyotome, Sclerotome, Epithelial, 100%). Mixed: `HH17/16|Early LPM` = Skeletogenic LPM 59% / Venous-pole spl. 29% / Cardiopharyngeal LPM 11% (no HH17/16 population is called Early LPM any more); `HH17/16|Somatic` = Skeletogenic LPM 49% / Contamination 19% / Cardiopharyngeal LPM 18% / PRRX1+/FOXF1-low mes. 13%; `HH17/16|Dorsal-biased LPM` = Dorsal-biased LPM 78% / BARX1+ 9% / Venous-pole spl. 8% / Skeletogenic LPM 5%; `HH21|Dorsal sub-epi.` = Ventral spl. 44% / Dorsal sub-epi. 33% / Skeletogenic LPM 22%; `HH21|Splanchnic` = BARX1+ 89% / Venous-pole spl. 10%; `HH21|Syringeal` = Syringeal 95% / other 5%; `HH21|Ventral spl.` = Ventral spl. 72% / Venous-pole spl. 23% / other 5%; `HH25|Somatic` = Ventral spl. 46% / Skeletogenic LPM 45% / Dorsal sub-epi. 9%; `HH25|Ventral spl.` = Ventral spl. 90% / Venous-pole spl. 8%.
  - Read the composition table, never the pool names. A name-based mapping is wrong for `HH17/16|Early LPM`, `HH21|Dorsal sub-epi.`, `HH21|Ventral spl.` and `HH25|Somatic`.
  - The peak SET is unaffected: the 283,818-peak union is the union over pools, whatever the pools are called. The four callable pools with no reproducible peak set (`HH13|Early LPM`, `HH13|Sclerotome`, `HH17/16|Epicardial mes.`, `HH21|Somatic`) are in the composition table but have no B1 bar.
- B2 point size = number of pseudo-replicates (8–24). B4 annotation breakdown: Promoter 17.5% / Exonic 16.1% / Intronic 35.9% / Distal 30.4%.

### Panel C
- Cohort (exporter rules): embryos binned by the sidecar's `stage_anchor` (so g01, g08 and g20, which carry the literal stage HH16/HH17, sit in HH17/16); sides through the shared firmness rule (same-side duplicates G3_unfiltered_donor0/3 excluded by name: one half in two libraries, no opposite half); every embryo that misses a row is logged by name with its reason (one side only, < 10 cells on a side, no isoform-informative read); each (stage, population, metric) has its own seeded generator (seed `[0, crc32("stage|population|metric")]`), so a cohort change at one stage cannot move another stage's CI or permutation p. HH17/16 (PITX2_mean): BARX1⁺ 8/9 Left, CI [+0.27, +8.06]; Cardiopharyngeal LPM 10/12, [+1.55, +6.52]; Skeletogenic LPM 13/15, [+0.66, +2.88]; Dorsal-biased LPM 7/11, [−0.70, +1.90] (the three inference/skeletogenic points filled; dorsal control open).
- The `PITX2c_usage` metric is exported but not plotted; it is sparse and its per-embryo log ratios sit near the pseudocount (±7–10), so its resampled CI bounds jump between a few discrete values — do not quote its CIs as precise.
- **BARX1⁺ HH17/16 median log₂ is set by the pseudocount.** Its median log₂(L/R) is +7.59 (off-scale, drawn at the +5 ceiling) while the median L − R difference is +0.19 CP10K: several embryos have R halves near 0 PITX2, so the ratio is set by the `eps` pseudocount in `export_laterality_trajectory.py`, not by biology. Quote BARX1⁺ HH17/16 by its direction count (8/9 Left) and median L − R, never by the log₂ value.
- Glyphs: a Right-lateralized in-range point is a red **square** (house L = blue circle / R = red square). The only such point is Skeletogenic LPM HH25.
- y = median across embryos of the per-embryo log₂(L/R) of total-PITX2 snRNA CP10K, with a bootstrap 95% CI (resample embryos with replacement, B = 2000).
- Two criteria, kept distinct: the point colour is the bootstrap CI-excludes-0 test; the sign-test p quoted in the legend is a separate, more conservative per-embryo direction test. They usually agree but can diverge (Cardiopharyngeal LPM @ HH13: 5/5 Left, CI excludes 0, sign p = 0.0625 — the n=5 floor; Skeletogenic LPM @ HH25: CI sits entirely below 0 so the point is coloured Right, sign p = 0.125 at n=4), so a coloured point is not automatically sign-test significant.
- Effect axis capped at +5 via `coord_cartesian` (not a hard scale limit, which would drop off-scale CIs): early-stage log₂FC inflates when R ≈ 0 (e.g. Cardiopharyngeal LPM HH13 ≈ +8.5). A point whose median is off the top is drawn as a ▲ at the +5 ceiling; a point whose CI top is clipped carries a small ▲ caret. CIs are bootstrap percentile CIs of a median over a skewed small-n distribution, so they are legitimately asymmetric around the point.
- Side basis: the canonical `d2n`-merged sidecar — chromatin `d2n` at HH17/16 and snRNA `d2l` at HH13/HH21/HH25. **At HH17/16 the RNA points are partly circular, not independent**: the sides are called from PITX2c-promoter accessibility, and PITX2c accessibility and PITX2 RNA covary in the same halves, so labelling the higher-PITX2c half "L" selects for L > R PITX2 RNA. The modality differs but the gene does not. The legend says so. The readout is a per-embryo direction statistic (pseudoreplication-robust); the flat dorsal control (computed identically) is a check on that selection, not proof it is absent.
- Off-axis populations (Contamination, Mesothelium, Venous-pole spl., PRRX1+/FOXF1-low mes.) are also Left-early but omitted on axis/lineage grounds (off the LPM-lineage axis); they remain in the exported TSV. NB the omission is not because the signal is weak — `PRRX1+/FOXF1-low mes.` is 6/6 Left at HH13 (p = 0.031) and 12/13 at HH17/16 (p = 0.0034).
- Inference-pool justification (moved from the legend): the syrinx lineage stays Left and is sign-test significant at the fate stages in every inference population except HH21 Syringeal (Ventral spl. 8/8 at HH21 and HH25, Syringeal 7/7 at HH25, BARX1⁺ 8/8 at HH21; sign p ≤ 0.016). The exception: HH21 Syringeal is 6/8, sign p = 0.29, CI spans 0 — the panel correctly draws it as a grey open (bilateral) point; the population becomes Left only at HH25. Skeletogenic LPM is Left at the trough (HH17/16 13/15, sign p = 0.0074, CI excludes 0), bilateral at HH21 (5/8, p = 0.73, CI spans 0) and weakly R-leaning at HH25 (0/4, p = 0.125, CI below 0) — excluded on fate-stage bilaterality, not blanket symmetry. The dorsal control stays bilateral throughout (every CI spans 0). The filled HH17/16 points (BARX1⁺, Cardiopharyngeal LPM, Skeletogenic LPM) are not independent laterality evidence: their sides are PITX2c-chromatin calls (see Side basis above).

### Panel H (cis-memory-node specificity; used as Figure 5D)
- Universe (seeded-relabel substrate): the 79 Ventral_spl HH21 lateral DEGs (padj < 0.1; 60 L-up / 19 R-up, PITX2 included); PITX2 is the labeled reference, excluded from the competitor count. PITX2 + the two locus ncRNAs are set aside as non-independent — LOC113843539, the antisense (−-strand) RNA overlapping the PITX2 3′UTR, and LOC106017192, the +-strand PANCR-like RNA on PITX2's own strand. "Expressed at the trough" (HH17/16 POOL baseMean) is a reported context column, not a filter.
- *promoter* — each registered isoform TSS (GTF transcript) → the ArchR union peak containing that TSS (reproduces PITX2's canonical a/b/c promoter peaks; a gene's promoters = every such peak). **±1 kb rescue:** ArchR peaks are fixed-width (501 bp) and a called promoter element need not be centred on the TSS, so containment alone leaves 12 of the 79 responders with no promoter (11 of them within 800 bp of a peak, KDM7A and UBL3 missing by ONE base, on elements already fitted genome-wide by `g4h_prep`; all clear the MIN_PEAK=10 floor at 22–655 trough fragments). Placement is two-pass: containment for every gene, then nearest-peak-within-±`PROMOTER_WINDOW` (default 1000 bp) applied **only** to genes with no containing peak, so a gene with a containing peak never gains an alternative-TSS peak. The `placement` / `tss_to_peak_bp` columns record which rule placed each row; `PROMOTER_WINDOW=0` gives containment only; `--selfcheck` covers the logic. Result: **105 unique promoter peaks / 107 gene–promoter rows** across **78** genes; only LOC119716870 stays unplaced (2,705 bp to the nearest peak); 75 independent competitor genes are tested. The **peak family's BH denominator is the 105 unique peaks**, not the 107 association rows — one trial per physical element. PITX2c q = **0.0030**; SMOC2 0.25; **independent competitors are 0 in all four family × design cells**.
- *reference flags* — `is_ref` is GENE-level (any of PITX2's three promoter peaks) and `is_pitx2c` is PEAK-level (the c promoter alone). Consumers that mean "the fig03 PITX2c promoter" MUST use `is_pitx2c`; averaging all three `is_ref` peaks dilutes the c effect with the a/b bilateral control.
- *lateralized* — the promoter's trough L/R is the `g4h_prep` DESeq2 effect, sign-oriented so +log2FC = Left-open ("rectified"), under the scan's PRIMARY family × design (default: per-promoter-PEAK family, unpaired = fig03 panel C's primary design), with BH-FDR restricted to the response-promoter family. The scan also writes the per-GENE family (min-P per gene, BH over genes, evaluated at the min-P promoter row) and the paired design; all four q's are in `figures/data/grn_memory_node_promoter_scan.tsv` (`padj_family_*`, `padj_genefamily_*`, `padj_primary`, `primary_family`/`primary_design`).
- A gene *competes* if any of its promoters is lateralized (family-FDR < 0.1) in the concordant direction (its HH21 DEG side); discordant promoters are enumerated.
- Panel: one volcano — x = trough L/R effect (log2FC), y = primary-family FDR significance (−log10, dashed line = FDR 0.1); colour = HH21 DEG side (blue L-up / red R-up). Significance, not magnitude, drives the read. Annotated with four labels: PITX2c, **PITX2a/b as ONE merged "PITX2a/b promoters" label** (they sit ~0.03 log2FC apart, so the label is placed at their midpoint — both points are still drawn), the +5.4 kb element (◆, PITX2's own distal element — reference, not competitor) and the nearest independent gene (SMOC2, n.s.). **Caveats (read with the panel):**
1. *Design-dependence of the positive call.* The PITX2c call is unpaired-only (fig03 panel C's primary design): under the paired design **at the 50-nucleus tier these trough tables use**, the fit's own record — `figures/data/g4h_cohorts.tsv`, written by `g4h_prep` — is **2 complete pairs / 4 halves / 1 residual df** (ok status; the unpaired trough cell is 11 L / 6 R halves, the split-tube halves 17-5/17-6 included with both aliquots summed). **Complete pairs only**: `g4h_prep_trough_lr.py` drops singleton genotypes before the paired fit, matching its sibling `beeswarm_deseq2_stats.R` (a singleton's own genotype coefficient absorbs its single sample, contributing nothing to the L/R contrast while potentially deflating the dispersion estimate; singletons belong to the unpaired/inclusive arm). Consequence when reading the scan TSV: the paired count floor runs over the 4 paired halves alone, so **26 of the 105 response-promoter peaks — the PITX2a promoter among them — are untested in the paired arm** (explicit NaN; the paired family BH runs over the tested peaks, and the scan prints the untested count). The HH21 module below runs at the 20-nucleus tier, where the same stage has 8 pairs. **Caution: the `h6` balanced paired fit (`h6_paired_deseq2_HH17_16_balanced_summary.tsv`: G2_genotype_02, g08) is a different step with a different cohort rule that coincidentally also reads "2 genotypes / 1 residual df"** — cite `g4h_cohorts.tsv` for this trough fit, never the h6 summary. Either way: no power, not evidence of absence (PITX2c paired q 0.991). The four-cell grid is printed by the scan so the family/design choice can never silently decide the headline.
2. Within the promoters the L-open chromatin is c-specific (a/b bilateral); the +5.4 kb element is PITX2's own cis-landscape, not an independent gene, and it is not pre-lateralized at the trough.
3. *Coverage:* 78 / 79 genes testable (see universe). Circularity: the trough sides are PITX2c-accessibility-defined, so the PITX2c point is circular by construction; competitor promoters are not (given sides fixed by PITX2c, is a second locus lateralized?).
4. The two +5.4 kb LOCs reproduce `g4h`'s PITX2-locus-neighbour finding with the tighter ±300 bp / TSS-containing rule rather than the ±50 kb window.
5. *Why FDR, not a naive CI:* 3 L-up + 1 R-up independent genes (SMOC2, PFKFB3, STARD13; KCNH7) are nominally p < 0.05 but 0 survive the response-family FDR — a raw effect-size strip or nominal-CI forest would falsely read as ~4 lateralized genes.

### HH21 module positive control + NB mixed-model arm test (panel_hh21_hh1716_module)
- **Two-stage regeneration — the R script MUST be rerun whenever the counts change.** The per-half response-peak counts + offsets (`figures/data/hh21_hh1716_{response_counts,halfmeta}.tsv`) are emitted by a **cluster** job (`pipeline/run_hh21_module_v2.sh` → `figures/figS03/hh21_hh1716_module_test.py`, on the da_archr pseudobulks at the **20-nucleus** tier) and are manifest rows (`produced_by = hh21_module_v2`), so they travel with the other manifest tables and `scripts/verify_derived_data.py` sees them. The NB-GLMM output (`figures/data/hh21_hh1716_nb_glmm.tsv`) is produced **locally** by `figures/figS03/hh21_hh1716_nb_glmm.R` reading those two TSVs. **Contract: if `response_counts.tsv`/`halfmeta.tsv` are regenerated, rerun `Rscript figures/figS03/hh21_hh1716_nb_glmm.R` (needs r-lme4/r-glmmtmb/r-emmeans, provided by `envs/duckfig_portable.yml` env) AND re-render the panel — otherwise the panel's sign-flip dots (live from `module_scores.tsv`) and its GLMM subtitle (from a stale `nb_glmm.tsv`) would silently disagree.**
- **Arms.** The reference arm is the **PITX2c promoter PEAK alone** (`is_pitx2c`), not the mean over every `is_ref` peak (which would dilute the c-promoter effect with the a/b bilateral control). PITX2a/b are their own two-sided `PITX2ab_ctrl` arm, and PITX2's +5.4 kb element is held out of the L-up/R-up arms (`PITX2_locus_other`), so the response module is the **75 independent responder genes / 101 promoter peaks** panel H counts. Current values (floor-20 tier, 8 pairs per stage): HH21 PITX2c 8/8 (label-swap p 0.0039), a/b control 4/8 (two-sided p 0.82), L-up 6/8 (p 0.051), directionality 7/8 (p 0.031); HH17/16 PITX2c 8/8 (side-defining, not a test), a/b 5/8 (p 0.65), directionality 5/8 (p 0.57). **Sign-unstable trough directionality:** the HH17/16 module directionality is ±0.008 — null (both CIs span zero) and close enough to zero that the two offset conventions give it OPPOSITE signs. `hh21_hh1716_nb_glmm.R`'s offset-stability guard records `sign_unstable = TRUE` for a converged pair that straddles zero **within** the 0.1 tolerance **and** whose CIs both contain zero, and `panel_hh21_hh1716_module.py` prints "directionality ≈ 0 (sign not determined)" for such a row rather than a direction. A sign flip with a gap > 0.1, or one where a CI excludes zero, is fatal. Only the two HH17/16 glmmTMB rows carry the flag.
- **Model:** `count ~ arm*side + (1+int_LL|embryo) + (1+side|peak_id) + offset(log·)`, NB (glmer.nb primary + glmmTMB cross-check), size-factor AND total-fragment offsets. The embryo INTERACTION slope (`int_LL`) makes the embryo the inferential unit for the L-up−R-up directionality. **On the locus-excluded module that slope is not identifiable at either stage in any engine × offset** (the `singular` column reads FALSE on every committed row because the row records the BACKED-OFF ladder rung the fit fell to, not the singular full model it started from — `ranef_structure` is where that shows), so no directionality p is embryo-calibrated: the estimates (+0.14 log2 at HH21, ≈ 0 at the trough) are reported with `embryo_calibrated = FALSE` and p suppressed to NA, and the exact label-swap permutation is the anchor. With PITX2's +5.4 kb element left inside the L-up arm the HH21 slope IS identifiable (+0.19 log2 [+0.01,+0.37], p 0.019) — i.e. identifiability comes from PITX2's own element, which is why the hold-out matters. `nb_glmm.tsv` flags this per row via `df_used` + `embryo_calibrated`; the run prints a banner when HH21 is uncalibrated (recorded, not fatal).
- **Coding-invariance:** treatment-coded `int_LL` is the fitted primary; a sum-coded reference-invariant slope (`int_c`) is a documented sensitivity (its small variance component trips lme4's whole-fit `isSingular`); it has not been derived on the locus-excluded module (where the slope is singular in any case).

### HH21 promoter-motif analysis (no panel — analysis-only, docs are the consumer)

Three engines here are **cluster-executed analysis code, not figure code**, and none of their 21
`figures/data/hh21_{trans,seq,seq1,smad234,dinuc,trinuc}_*` outputs feeds a panel. Registered in
`figures/data/README.md`; **not** in `pipeline/derived_data_manifest.tsv`, so
`scripts/verify_derived_data.py` does not guard their freshness (the validator keys on
cell-type-annotation columns, which none of these carry).

- **`hh21_trans_motif.py` — v1, SUPERSEDED.** Promoter = the ArchR peak *containing* a TSS. Kept
  as the recorded v1 negative; that definition gates the gene universe on having an OPEN promoter.
  Still owns the shared `fit_panel()` GLM helper that v2/v3 imports.
- **`hh21_trans_motif_seq.py` — v2/v3, CURRENT.** TSS-anchored sequence promoters. Modes:
  `prep` (cluster) / `test` / `partition` / `tail` / `bound` / `dinuc_null` (local).
  `bound` refits the headline contrast under seven specifications so the write-up can quote the
  RANGE of upper limits; the single HC0 limit is the narrowest of the seven, and
  quoting it alone reads as more precision than the data support.
- **`hh21_smad234_dinuc_null.R`** — k-let-preserving shuffle null (`SHUFFLE_K=2|3`).
- **`hh21_trans_motif_scan.R`** — shared motif scanner; duck ZJU1.0 only (BSgenome and the PWM
  background `BG` are duck-specific and must change together).

**Naming trap:** `hh21_seq1_*` is the **single**-TSS variant and is **DEFINITIVE**; bare
`hh21_seq_*` is the **merged**-TSS **sensitivity** variant. The `1` is a variant tag, not a
version number, and it is the opposite of the usual "bare name = canonical" convention.

**Two-stage regeneration contract.** `prep` + both R scans run on the cluster
(`pipeline/run_hh21_trans_motif{,_seq}.sh`) and are ferried back; every GLM runs locally. **If
`prep` is rerun, the scans AND every local mode must be rerun** — `prep` writes the BEDs, so a
changed window definition silently pairs a fresh map with stale motif counts. `_gene_table()`
guards the obvious form of this (map/scan gene-set equality, and single-vs-merged scan shape),
but it cannot detect a scan regenerated from the *same* genes with different coordinates.

`figures/figS03/` must be present in the working tree next to `pipeline/` when the cluster stages run.
