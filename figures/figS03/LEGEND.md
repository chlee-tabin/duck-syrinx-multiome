# Figure S10: snATAC-seq QC and robustness of the PITX2-locus laterality

**Supplementary to Figure 5: library and peak-set QC, per-subcluster PITX2-RNA laterality trajectories, a depth census with a matched-power control, and cross-subcluster L/R-DEG specificity.**

**(A)** Per-library snATAC QC (A1-A7): dodged bars compare initial (dark) and QC-filtered (light; TSS ≥ 2, high-quality fragments ≥ 1000) nuclei. A1 nuclei per library; A2 raw reads per nucleus; A3 duplicate rate; A4 high-quality fragments per nucleus; A5 TSS-fragment fraction; A6 fraction of reads in peaks over the ArchR union peak set (Granja et al. 2021); A7 AMULET doublet rate (Thibodeau et al. 2021; 3.4-7.8%, median ~5.8%).

**(B)** ArchR peak-set QC (283,818 fixed-width 501-bp peaks): B1 union peaks detected per current stage × LPM population (Figure 4B vocabulary; detected = ≥ 1 fragment in ≥ 5% of the group's nuclei, at least 3 nuclei; n = nuclei per group); B2 detected peaks vs nuclei per group; B3 peak-score distribution; B4 genomic annotation (Promoter 17.5% / Exonic 16.1% / Intronic 35.9% / Distal 30.4%). The chromatin-arbitrated left/right side calls behind Figure 5 — reducing snRNA-only mapping conflicts from 12/52 to 0/52 — are provided in full as Table S5.

**(C)** Per-subcluster PITX2-RNA laterality trajectories: one facet per LPM subcluster; x = stage (HH13 → HH17/16 → HH21 → HH25), y = across-embryo median of per-embryo log₂(L/R) total-PITX2 snRNA expression (CP10K), bootstrap 95% CI; filled = CI excluding 0 (lateralized; Left = blue circle, Right = red square), open grey = CI spanning 0 (bilateral). The metric is RNA; side labels are chromatin-arbitrated at HH17/16, so HH17/16 points are descriptive rather than an independent test. Axis clipped at +5/−2; off-scale values drawn as edge glyphs. Facets are grouped by pool role: inference pool / syrinx lineage (Early LPM, Cardiopharyngeal LPM, BARX1+, Ventral spl., Syringeal — the IRX1⁺ syrinx precursor), Skeletogenic LPM (excluded), and dorsal control (Dorsal-biased LPM at HH17/16; Dorsal sub-epi. at HH21/HH25).

**(D)** Depth census: nuclei and fragments per embryo-half × stage × compartment × side (one point = one embryo-half) on the ≥20-cell pseudobulks — the same cohort as E, Figure S11A and the HH17/16–HH25 lanes of the Figure 5 pileup — showing where observations are sparse (ventral HH13; dorsal HH25). Left = blue circle, Right = red square.

**(E)** Matched-power control, element-resolved (PITX2a/b/c promoters; a +5.4 kb putative distal enhancer; a representative upstream-desert Right-drift peak): every ventral stage subsampled to a common n = 8 L / 6 R (the HH13 floor) and the L/R log₂(mean CPM) recomputed by bootstrap. At matched depth, the PITX2c promoter is Left at all four stages (+1.4 to +1.6) and the +5.4 kb element at all four stages (+0.9 to +1.3; its 90% interval only just excludes 0 at HH17/16). The PITX2a/b promoters are near-bilateral except PITX2b at HH13 (+0.75), and the desert peak is Right at HH21 and HH25. Matched and full-depth estimates agree (PITX2c HH13: +1.57 vs +1.56).

**(F)** Cross-subcluster L/R-DEG overlap at HH25: UpSet of L- and R-biased DE-gene sets across four populations — Ventral spl. 214 L / 351 R; Syringeal 0 L / 2 R; Skeletogenic LPM (excluded-population control) 0 L / 0 R; Dorsal sub-epi. (bilateral control) 1 L / 15 R. The lateralized program is overwhelmingly Ventral spl.-specific (213 of 214 Left genes are Ventral spl.-only; CSMD1 and FAM241A are the only genes shared three-ways among the Right sets), and the dorsal control's small set is right-leaning (Figure 5B).
