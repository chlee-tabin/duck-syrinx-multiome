# Figure S14: The E6-E11 scRNA-seq dataset: atlas, lineage correspondence, and ESR1 as the laterality-relevant estrogen receptor

**(A)** UMAP of the integrated, ambient-corrected atlas colored by developmental stage (E6, E9, E11).

**(B)** The same UMAP colored by cell type: LPM mesenchyme subpopulations for mesodermal cells, broad classes (endoderm, endothelium, immune) for the rest, plus a contamination class (ribosomal/erythroid nuclei) retained for transparency.

**(C)** Cross-stage correspondence between the multiome series (HH13-HH25) and this dataset (E6-E11), as a transit map (rows = populations, columns = stages; node area ∝ cell number): black edges = reciprocal-best replicability matches, thin grey = weaker within-platform links, magenta = cross-platform links, dotted magenta = direct HH25-E9 reciprocal-best matches — among them ventral splanchnic to syringeal chondrocyte — situating this dataset as the continuation of the multiome series. Correspondence is transcriptional similarity, not lineage tracing.

**(D)** Percentage of ESR1+ cells per embryo across the full HH13-E11 window: ESR1 turns on at HH25 in the syrinx-fated ventral splanchnic / syringeal fibroblast track — coincident with the second wave of PITX2 — and is maintained through E11.

**(E)** Per-embryo pseudobulk ESR1 expression in left vs right halves, paired within embryo, by stage and population (an expression-level endpoint complementary to the detection-fraction endpoint of Figure S17); sex on the x-axis, dot area ∝ cells per side (embryos with ≥10 cells on both sides). ESR1 is left-biased in the ventral splanchnic / syringeal fibroblast lineage at E9 and E11, with a left trend already at HH25; the dorsal sub-epithelial control is flat at HH25, shows only a non-significant left lean at E9, and no dorsal sub-epithelial cluster is detected at E11.

**(F)** Cycling-cell fraction for the same populations at E9 and E11: at E9 the syringeal chondrocyte population cycles more on the left; by E11 the left proliferation advantage is confined to males (descriptive; 4 male and 4 female donors), consistent with estrogen suppressing left proliferation in females.

**(G)** ESRRG control: its apparently broad expression is dominated by intronic signal spread across its ~400 kb gene body, consistent with an intron artifact of internal priming — leaving ESR1 as the laterality-relevant estrogen receptor.
