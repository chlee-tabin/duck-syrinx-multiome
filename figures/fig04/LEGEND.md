# Figure S11: Cross-species PITX2-locus laterality (duck vs chick)

**The duck PITX2c left/right chromatin asymmetry is a focal, isoform-specific, chromatin-gated feature of the PITX2 locus that is bilateral/null in chick at the orthologous locus and is built on conserved cis-elements — duck laterality reflects a change in chromatin state, not duck-specific sequence.**

**(A)** Duck PITX2-locus per-peak L/R accessibility landscape: per-peak DESeq2 L/R log2 fold change across the PITX2-locus window (upstream edge at C4orf32/FAM241A; Welsh et al. 2015), gene-model track at left (FAM241A, PITX2, ELOVL6; LOC106016802 (PLAYRR?; Welsh et al. 2015) and LOC106017192 (PANCR?; Gore-Panter et al. 2016) as candidate orthologs, not asserted). Seven tracks — ventral HH13/HH17-16/HH21/HH25 and dorsal HH17-16/HH21/HH25 — each labelled with its embryo-half L/R n. Tile fill = L/R log2 fold change (blue = Left, red = Right); * = locus-adjusted padj < 0.1; grey = peak absent (below the pooled-count floor); white = tested-but-bilateral; GC%/CpG-island strip alongside. The PITX2c core (plus the 3′/PANCR block) is consistently Left-biased in the ventral columns; the dorsal columns carry no significant call (underpowered at 12L/5R, 6L/5R, 5L/7R; point estimates trend the same direction); only PITX2c — not PITX2a/b — is asymmetric, and the focal L-bias sits on the densest CpG island in the window (the PITX2 promoter cluster). Replicate unit = embryo-half.

**(B)** Orientation-free PITX2 laterality magnitude, four readouts: per embryo, the half-vs-half divergence of each readout above its own within-embryo cell-shuffle null (gap = obs − null), with the left/right label discarded — non-circular, so every embryo of both species contributes. Rows: ATAC at the PITX2c/PITX2a/PITX2b promoters (null-corrected |Δlog2 FRiP|) and total PITX2 RNA (null-corrected |Δmean cp10k|); stages HH13/HH17-16/HH21/HH25 (chick HH15-16 at the HH17/16 tick; no chick HH21 column). Duck = black, chick = teal; open circles, area ∝ min-half nuclei; bar = per-stage median; dashed line = own null (oriented view: Figure S12G). The PITX2c ATAC and total-PITX2 RNA divergences are duck-specific; PITX2a and PITX2b diverge in neither species — the within-locus controls isolating the asymmetry to PITX2c.

**(C)** PITX2-locus motif composition at HH25 (wave-2), DUCK | CHICK dot plot. Rows = six curated motifs with sequence logos (SMAD5, SMAD2-3-4, NKX6-1, PITX2, GATA4, ISL1), plus the GC-immune SBE (AGAC) raw count as a SMAD5 sub-row. Columns = the named PITX2 elements (PITX2a/b/c promoters, +5.4 kb), the aggregated HH25 L-biased cis-block (PITX2c and +5.4 kb also broken out), the distal R-desert landmark, and a GC- and locale-matched bilateral background (non-lateralized peaks within ±50 kb). Dot size = presence ratio (fraction of the column's peaks carrying ≥1 site); fill = mean motif-instance count.
