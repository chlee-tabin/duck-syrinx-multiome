# Figure S7: Experimental design, genotype demultiplexing, and snRNA-seq cell type atlas

**Multiome profiling of 52 duck embryos across 5 developmental stages yields a 177K-nucleus atlas of the syrinx and surrounding tissues.**

**(A)** Embryo × library pooling matrix: 53 designed embryos (rows, HH25 through HH13) × 12 multiomic libraries (columns); Left = blue circle, Right = red square. Each embryo was bisected along the midline and its halves distributed to different libraries; PILOTGEX4A/B (PG4A/PG4B) are technical replicates of the same side; embryos 16-4, 16-5, 17-A through 17-D, 21-1 through 21-4 and 17-KC-1 lack paired libraries; embryos 17-5 and 17-6 were sequenced as left halves only, from one suspension split between GEX04 and GEX0S-duck.

**(B)** Embryo-identity validation cascade: per-library CellBender ambient-RNA correction (Fleming et al. 2023), cellSNP-lite variant genotyping (Huang & Huang 2021) and scDblFinder doublet scoring; vireo demultiplexing applied independently to each of three sequencing pools (Figure S8P); then three orthogonal checks — cross-pool pseudobulk allele-frequency correlation, sex-chromosome (W/Z) UMI ratios confirming single-embryo genotypes, and a laterality check of left/right half assignment — together confirming 52 of 53 designed embryos. QC filters: genes [200, 8000], UMIs ≥ 500, mito ≤ 5%, vireo singlets only. From 181,159 vireo singlets, the Leiden-clustered atlas (Figure 4A) comprises 176,933 nuclei.

**(C)** Discriminating markers across all 16 cell types (37 genes; dot size = fraction of nuclei expressing, shade = mean expression), grouped: pan-mesenchymal (SNAI2, COL1A1), sclerotome (PAX1, PAX9), splanchnic LPM (FOXF1, GATA4, ZFPM2, TBX5, TBX4), LPM mesenchyme (KIF26B, NFIA), dermomyotome (PAX3, CDH4), neural (SOX2, PTPRZ1), neural crest (COL25A1, TFAP2B), differentiated neuron (MYT1L), ventral neural (ARX), epithelial (EPCAM, MECOM), pharyngeal endoderm (FOXI2, TFAP2E), otic placode (AGR3), endothelial (PECAM1, FLI1), muscle (MYOD1, ACTA2), notochord (COL8A1, SHH), laterality (PITX2, LEFTY1), BMP signaling (BMP4, BMP7, ID1), and proliferation (MKI67, TOP2A).

**(D)** Pseudobulk PCA of the mesenchymal subset (LPM + splanchnic mesenchyme) by genotype: developmental stage and sex separate along rotated axes rather than cleanly along PC1 or PC2. One point per genotype; shape = pre-inference design pool (filled circles = known stage; open triangles = HH21/16 pool; open diamonds = HH17/16 pool; open squares = unknown stage); convex hulls = inferred stage groups (HH25 green, HH21 orange, HH17-16 purple — combining HH17, HH17/16, HH16 — HH13 light green). Female and male genotypes separate along the rotated sex axis, anchored by the sex-chromosome ratios in (B).
