## Demultiplexing methods (per-pool detail)

53 embryos (HH13–HH25) were bisected along the midline. Left and right halves were distributed across 12 snRNA-seq libraries in **3 sequencing pools**, then computationally demultiplexed using genetic variants (vireo) in **4 demultiplexing runs** ("Pool 4" below is a reference-based re-genotyping of Pool 1's PILOT4 libraries, not a fourth sequencing pool). **Two** designed embryos are undetected — 17-D (~60 nuclei, below confidence threshold) and one G3 embryo (undetectable in both the 2024 ARC and 2026 GEX pipelines) — and the count returns to 52 because the g26 over-merge was split into two embryos during consolidation.

### Library-to-pool mapping

| Pool | Libraries | Embryos | Strategy | Rationale |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|-----------|
| Pool 1 | GEX05, GEX06, GEX07, GEX08, PG3, PG4B | HH25 (25-5..14), HH21 (21-5..8), HH17 (17-9..12), HH16 (16-6..9), HH13 (13-5..14) | Library-pair decomposition | 6 libraries with overlapping embryos; suffix pairs (-5/-6, -7/-8, -3/-2) define independent sub-pools for vireo, unified via merged 37-donor reference VCF |
| Pool 2 | GEX03-1A, GEX03-1B, GEX03-2 | HH21 (21-13..16), HH17 (17-Md-13/14, 17-KC-1/2/3), HH16 (16-Md-1, 16-KC-1) | Breed-aware variant selection | Pool contains both Mallard and Khaki Campbell breeds; universal variant set loses breed-discriminative positions |
| Pool 3 | GEX04, GEX0S-duck | HH17 (17-5..8), HH21 (21-1..4), HH16 (16-4/5) | De novo variant discovery (cellSNP mode 2b, 173K variants) | Pool mixed with chick nuclei from a different experiment; universal duck variants have zero discriminative power for chick contamination; de novo discovery required |
| Pool 4 | PG4A, PG4B | HH25 (25-11..14), HH17 (17-A..D), HH13 (13-11..14) | Reference-based vireo using Pool 1's VCF | PILOT libraries are technical counterparts to GEX05-08; all donors cross-link to Pool 1 embryos (0 new); validates Pool 1 assignments |

PG3 and PG4B appear in both Pool 1 and Pool 4 because the same physical nuclei are genotyped in both contexts; Pool 4 provides independent validation of Pool 1 assignments.

### Per-library QC (applied before demultiplexing)

1. **CellBender** — ambient RNA correction per library, filtered to cellranger-called barcodes.
2. **cellSNP-lite** — variant genotyping at 665K universal positions (Pools 1, 2, 4) or de novo (Pool 3).
3. **scDblFinder** — transcriptome-based doublet scoring per library (QC metadata; not used for filtering — doublet removal is vireo-based).

### Pool 1 — library-pair decomposition (29 embryos)

The 6 Pool-1 libraries contain overlapping embryos (e.g. embryo 25-5 has nuclei in both GEX05 and PG3). Direct vireo on all 6 would confound donors across libraries. Instead: (1) split nuclei by barcode suffix into 3 pairs (-5/-6, -7/-8, -3/-2); (2) run vireo de novo on each pair (chunked multi-seed, nInit=50); (3) build a merged 37-donor reference VCF; (4) classify all nuclei against it. Result: 37 donors → **29 unique embryos** (some donors are left/right halves of the same embryo). The **dp500 rescue** — donor 25-8 (GEX05 only) recovered via depth-filtered classification — is booked *on top of* the 29 during consolidation (+1, alongside g32 and g35).

> **Boundary note (embryo accounting).** The **29** counts the library-pair donors only. figS01 panel P's **main pool = 33 embryos** adds the consolidation recoveries: dp500_donor29 (+1), g32 (+1), g35 (+1) → 32, and the g26 (17-A..D) over-merge **split into two embryos** (+1) → 33. The 17-Md-13 G6↔G2 bridge embryo (g20) is *inside* the 29, not an addition. The genotype SSOT (`references/genotype_metadata.tsv`) has **52 distinct embryos = 33 (main) + 10 (GEX03/G2) + 9 (GEX04+GEX0S/G3)**.

### Pool 2 — breed-aware variants (10 embryos)

GEX03 contains embryos from two genetically distinct breeds (Mallard, Khaki Campbell). The universal 665K variant set, developed from Mallard reference, loses breed-discriminative positions; pool-specific variant discovery preserves KC-specific alleles. Result: 12 raw vireo donors → 10 unique embryos (2 pair-merged: G2_genotype_06 + G2_genotype_12 = 17-Md-13, confirmed by GT correlation r=0.91 on the combined 86×86 matrix `gt_distance_combined_corr.tsv`; the G2-only 12×12 matrix behind figS01 panel Q gives r=0.77 for the same pair — different variant sets, both committed).

### Pool 3 — de novo variant discovery (9 duck embryos)

GEX04 and GEX0S-duck were part of an experiment that also included chick samples. The 665K universal duck variants have zero discriminative power for chick nuclei, causing vireo to shatter donors. De novo variant discovery (cellSNP mode 2b) identifies 173K pool-specific positions separating duck from chick. Result: 11 vireo donors → 9 duck embryos (1 chick contamination excluded, 1 noise donor at 6 nuclei excluded).

### Pool 4 — reference-based cross-linking (0 new embryos)

PG4A and PG4B (PILOT4 technical replicates) contain the same embryos as GEX05-08 but sequenced independently. Reference-based vireo using Pool 1's VCF assigns all PILOT4 nuclei to existing Pool 1 donors, validating Pool 1 assignments. Result: 18 donors, all cross-linking to Pool 1 embryos; one candidate (G9_donor4, ~60 nuclei) excluded as ambient contamination (het=0.613, AF r=0.88 with library ambient profile).

### Cross-pool donor consolidation (→ 52 embryos)

1. **PILOT3 pseudobulk AF correlation** (r > 0.80): merges PILOT3 donors with their GEX counterparts (e.g. PG3 g32 = GEX07 component of embryo 13-5).
2. **Pool 4 barcode concordance**: all 18 Pool 4 donors map to Pool 1 embryos (0 new).
3. **Pool 2 → Pool 1 bridge**: 17-Md-13 identified in both Pool 2 (GEX03) and Pool 1 (GEX08) via suffix pattern matching.
4. **Over-merge reclassification**: one Pool 1 donor (g26, originally 17-C) was an over-merge of two embryos (one male PILOT4 + one female HH13); split into g26_split0 / g26_split1 using Pool 4 barcode concordance.
5. **HH13 combined recovery**: PILOT + GEX libraries combined for vireo (PG3+GEX07 at N=15, PG4A/B+GEX08 at N=19) to recover GEX-side nuclei for HH13-stage embryos too similar for library-pair separation.

Final: 52 embryos (29 Pool 1 + 10 Pool 2 + 9 Pool 3, plus 4 recovered via consolidation; Pool 4 adds 0 new). Two designed embryos undetected: 17-D (insufficient nuclei, ~60, ambient signature) and one G3 embryo (undetectable in both pipelines). Demultiplexing output 181,159 vireo singlets → 176,933 nuclei in the clustered atlas | 12 libraries | 52 embryos | 5 stages (HH13, HH16, HH17, HH21, HH25).
