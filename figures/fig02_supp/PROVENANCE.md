## Panel F (HH17/16 left/right cell composition)

Panel **F** (`panel_F_trough_composition.png`) asks how much of the right-biased AHR / CHRM2 signal in the HH17/16 inference pool is a shift in the Cardiopharyngeal LPM / BARX1+ balance between the halves rather than a per-subcluster expression change. AHR and CHRM2 are the two right-biased HH17/16 paired-DESeq2 pool DEGs, the same two genes shown in `fig03` panel B (0 L / 2 R at HH17/16); legend F points to Table S6 for the fold changes.

**Spec (as rendered).** One row of three plots; panel 2343 × 705 px (7.81 × 2.35 in) at 300 dpi, exactly panel E's width. Left: per paired embryo, Cardiopharyngeal LPM share of pool **nuclei**, Left (blue circle) vs Right (red square), grey line per embryo, black median ticks, one annotation line "9/13 R > L, p = 0.09". Right: **one per-embryo dot-strip plot per gene, AHR then CHRM2 (`DECOMP_GENES`), each with its own y axis** ("Right − Left expression (CPM)" on the AHR axis; AHR spans about −120 … 280, CHRM2 −200 … 530 CPM). In each, two columns: "composition" (dark points, fill 0.25, black edge) and "within subcluster" (light points, fill 0.78), 13 embryos each; a thin grey line joins the two parts of one embryo; a black horizontal tick marks the mean over embryos; a dotted line marks 0; above each column the count "k/13 > 0" (embryos whose part is above zero); the gene name (italic) above the counts. **No percentages anywhere on the panel** and no key (the x tick labels name the two parts). Point placement is deterministic: embryo i in genotype order sits at offset `linspace(−0.10, 0.10, 13)[i]` from both column centres (no random jitter; two renders are byte-identical). Text sizes are the shared `apply_layout_fonts()` ones used before (7 pt annotations, gene names and axis label; 6 pt tick labels). Checks before anything is drawn: `check_main_table` (unchanged; see below); `recompute_parts` recomputes every drawn point (each embryo's composition and within part) straight from the raw `umi_*` / `gene_umi_*` counts in the same rows (weights, rates, absent-side borrowing, symmetric split) and raises if any differs from the drawn value by more than 4× the worst-case 6-digit rounding (largest residual on the current table: 0.07 of that bound), or differs in sign; each mean tick is the mean of the drawn points and must equal the summary `composition_mean` / `within_mean`; each "k/13 > 0" count is counted from the drawn points and must equal the summary `composition_n_R_gt_L` / `within_n_R_gt_L`; the summary's `delta_mean` must equal the table mean and composition + within (kept as a guard, not drawn); the y range must bracket every point and 0; every text item must lie inside the canvas and each gene name above its counts; the share medians, R > L count and share p are checked as before. Mutation-tested (2026-10-15): a wrong count row (AHR composition 10, CHRM2 within 13), a missing count row, a summary mean off by 5 CPM, one embryo's composition moved into within in the main table, and one subcluster's `umi_R` × 1.5 each raise; the unmodified tables render.

**Cohort (reproduces the h2 POOL paired cohort).** Pseudobulk unit = (genotype, library, side) summed over the HH17/16 inference-pool subclusters (`POOLS["HH17/16"]["inference"]` = Cardiopharyngeal LPM + BARX1+, resolved through `get_pool_map`); counts clipped at 0 and rounded as in h2; floor = max(20, round(0.05 × median pooled nuclei)) = 20 (median 86); embryos (= genotypes) with both an L and an R pseudobulk at or above 20 pool nuclei (the test is n ≥ floor; the smallest kept half has exactly 20) = 13. Same-side duplicates (lane-loading-record tube group 17-B: donor0 / donor3, one left half split across GEX04 + GEX0S-duck) are pooled by h1 and are not pairs. Three more genotypes have both sides in h1 but one half below 20 pool nuclei (G3 donor5 R = 4 nuclei, G3 donor9 R = 16, g19 R = 3) and drop out, as in h2.

**Decomposition conventions (what legend F's "see Methods" points to).** Per paired embryo and gene, with k = subcluster (Cardiopharyngeal LPM, BARX1+) and s = half (L, R); U_k,s = total UMIs (all genes) of subcluster k's pseudobulk in half s, G_k,s = the gene's UMIs there.
- *Weights = UMI shares*: w_k,s = U_k,s / Σ_k U_k,s (the share of the half's pooled transcripts, which is what enters pooled CPM). The left plot shows the **nuclei** share n_k,s / Σ_k n_k,s (what a reader counts); both are in the TSV (medians: nuclei 0.42 → 0.62, UMI 0.37 → 0.61).
- *Rates*: c_k,s = 10⁶ G_k,s / U_k,s; pooled P_s = Σ_k w_k,s c_k,s = 10⁶ Σ_k G_k,s / Σ_k U_k,s (asserted).
- *Symmetric (midpoint) split, neither half the reference*: composition = Σ_k (w_k,R − w_k,L)(c_k,R + c_k,L)/2; within-subcluster = Σ_k (w_k,R + w_k,L)/2 · (c_k,R − c_k,L); composition + within = P_R − P_L exactly (asserted per embryo × gene in memory; the written TSV is re-read and checked to within its 6-significant-digit rounding, max residual 0.0008 CPM on the current build).
- *Absent-side borrowing*: a subcluster with no nuclei in one half has weight 0 there and takes the other half's rate (exact, because a weight-0 term adds nothing to that half's P); absent from both halves, it contributes 0 to both parts. In the cohort BARX1+ is absent from one half in 2 embryos (G2_genotype_04 R, G2_genotype_09 L): 1 L and 1 R borrowed row per gene.
- *Right plot* = each embryo's composition and within part (points); ticks = their means over the 13 embryos (additive in means: mean composition + mean within = mean difference); counts = embryos with that part > 0 (summary rows `composition_n_R_gt_L`, `within_n_R_gt_L`). With two subclusters the composition part is exactly (w_F,R − w_F,L) · [(c_F,R + c_F,L)/2 − (c_O,R + c_O,L)/2] (F = Cardiopharyngeal LPM, O = BARX1+), so its sign is the sign of the right-minus-left Cardiopharyngeal **UMI** share whenever the gene's mean rate is higher in Cardiopharyngeal LPM; on this build composition > 0 in the same 9 embryos for AHR and CHRM2, and they are the 9 embryos of the left plot's "9/13 R > L" (nuclei share) — the nuclei-share match is empirical, not an identity, so the legend does not state it. `composition_pct_of_delta` (43.3 / 47.5) stays in the summary as context; it is not drawn.
- *Enrichment fold* (legend's "about 7- and 13-fold"): per embryo, each subcluster's CPM over **both halves summed**, 10⁶ (G_k,L + G_k,R) / (U_k,L + U_k,R); fold = Cardiopharyngeal LPM / BARX1+; median over embryos. An embryo with 0 BARX1+ CPM (and > 0 Cardiopharyngeal) has an infinite fold that is **retained in the rank-based median** and counted (`fold_focal_over_other_n_zero_denominator`; CHRM2 has 1 such embryo: median 13.19× with it, 12.70× without — the safer number to quote). A 0/0 fold, or a subcluster with no nuclei in either half, is undefined: excluded from the median and counted (`fold_focal_over_other_n_undefined`, 0 for every gene on the current build). An infinite or undefined median is written as `inf` / `NaN` next to its counts instead of aborting the summary.
- *Tests*: two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank by exhaustive enumeration of all 2ⁿ sign assignments (`figures/_shared/signed_rank.py`): zero differences dropped and counted (`share_nuclei_n_zero_diff`, `cpm_lr_n_zero_diff_unborrowed`; all 0 on the current build), mid-ranks of |d| for ties, "zero" and "tied" judged with a relative tolerance of 10⁻¹⁰ × max|d| rather than exact float equality (rationally equal differences can differ by one ulp, e.g. 0.3 − 0.1 vs 0.5 − 0.3, and that noise must not decide a rank), p = min(1, 2 · min(P(T ≤ t), P(T ≥ t))) with T = sum of the ranks of the positive differences. Without ties this equals scipy's exact p (asserted at run time; true for every test on the current build, so no p value changed). scipy's `method="exact"` silently ignores ties (e.g. d = [1, 2, 4, −1, −4]: scipy 0.8125, enumeration 0.875), which is why it is no longer used. An undefined p (no non-zero difference) is written `NaN`; panel F refuses to print it. Share test: 13 pairs, no zero differences.
- `--self-check` runs five synthetic decomposition cases (random exact-sum, pure composition, pure within, one-side-absent borrow, both-absent) plus the signed-rank self-test (two hand-checked tied cases, zero-drop, all-zero, no-tie = scipy, 300 random tied cases vs a naive loop); both also run before every build.

**Legend numbers and robustness.** Legend F carries the causal link (AHR / CHRM2 ~7× / ~13× higher in Cardiopharyngeal LPM, 13/13 embryos); the per-part consistency is now on the panel itself ("k/13 > 0": composition 9/13 for both genes; within 11/13 AHR, 12/13 CHRM2; summary rows `composition_n_R_gt_L`, `within_n_R_gt_L`). Robustness (not on the panel or in the legend): bootstrap 95% CI of the composition share AHR ~0–71%, CHRM2 ~7–71%; leave-one-out AHR 34–53%, CHRM2 41–55%; left-/right-reference conventions AHR 36%/51%, CHRM2 36%/59%.

AHR (right plot, per embryo): composition mean +59.5 CPM (tick), 9/13 > 0; within mean +77.8 CPM (tick), 11/13 > 0; per-embryo range composition −121 … +277, within −77 … +227. **Where the within-subcluster part sits (context, not plotted; do not over-read).** Per-subcluster L/R CPM on embryos with an unborrowed rate on both sides: in Cardiopharyngeal LPM, AHR R > L in 11/13 (exact paired Wilcoxon p = 0.013) and CHRM2 11/13 (p = 0.021). In BARX1+ (11 embryos), AHR R > L in 7/11 (p = 0.24) and CHRM2 7/11 (p = 0.21) — **unresolved at this depth** (BARX1+ carries 89 L / 54 R AHR and 63 / 69 CHRM2 UMIs summed over the cohort). This is not evidence that BARX1+ lacks a right bias; the legend therefore does not attribute the within-subcluster part to either subcluster. **These per-subcluster counts are not an h2 subcluster result and should not be quoted as one:** they use the pool-floor cohort with no per-subcluster cell floor, whereas h2's own SUB scope on the same rerun (`h2_deg_summary.tsv`, paired) keeps only 10 (Cardiopharyngeal LPM) and 4 (BARX1+) paired genotypes and calls 0 DEGs in each. The citable HH17/16 result is the POOL one (13 paired embryos; AHR, CHRM2).

**Caveats.** Low-count genes in a small pool — summed over the 13 embryos, AHR has 401 L / 558 R UMIs, CHRM2 671 / 778, PITX2 350 / 128 — and several half-pools are small (6 of 26 halves hold 20–50 pool nuclei), so per-embryo shares are noisy; the panel is descriptive, not a test of the expression change. The paired DESeq2 on the same cohort calls AHR and CHRM2 the two right-biased pool DEGs at HH17/16 (log2FC −1.125, padj 0.0043; −1.347, padj 0.0201; contrast L vs R, so negative = higher on the right).

**Input provenance.** Built from the committed `figures/data/h1_pseudobulk_snrna_*` (the h1 run on the corrected design). On the pre-correction h1 the same step gives 15 paired embryos, share 0.32 → 0.60 (R > L 10/15, p = 0.095), AHR +114 (46%), CHRM2 +220 (47%), PITX2 −45 (1%) — the conclusion does not depend on the correction.

## Methodology trimmed from the legend

### Panel C
- Per-genotype within-pair fold magnitude = `(max(A,B) + ε) / (min(A,B) + ε)`, ε = 10⁻³ pseudocount, capped at 100×.
- Per-slot gate: ≥15 cells per slot and ≥2 paired genotypes per cluster.
- Same-side duplicates (d2l `firmness = same_side_dup`: `G3_unfiltered_donor0` / `G3_unfiltered_donor3`, the lane-loading-record split tube — one left half sequenced in GEX04 and GEX0S-duck) are dropped before pairing, as in Fig 4D: two libraries of the same half are not an L/R pair. The d2l set is cross-checked against `references/same_side_duplicate_genotypes.tsv` (`is_duplicate`) at import and raises on any difference. Effect, HH17/16 only (verified on the corrected data, 2026-10-14): the BARX1+ mean-PITX2 strip goes from 8 "pairs" (median fold 54.0×) to 6 (median 100×, the cap); the Dorsal-biased LPM strip from 11 to 9 pairs. The number of clusters passing the gate depends on the gate output and is not pinned in the legend.
- Dot positions within each strip are placed by deterministic beeswarm so that points at similar fold values fan horizontally instead of stacking, making the distribution (consistent signal vs single-outlier inflation) legible at a glance.

### Retired panel D — per-genotype L/R inference (now Table S4)
- Tint opacity saturates at ≥4× fold versus the partner library. Slots with <10 cells get a red diagonal hatch + italic metric values + a red "low N" label (an editorial cue to down-weight noisy slots).
- The genotype-to-embryo mapping intersects library membership (including a strict full-library-set match against the design), stage, and side calls with the wetlab L/R design. Footer rows compare inferred vs design (N_L, N_R) filtered by library-set equality so that cross-library-bridge embryos (e.g. 17-Md-13 at {GEX03-1, GEX08}) do not leak into unrelated blocks.
- **Three flag tiers:**
  - **Red** — block-level pair-constraint failure: the block's resolved L/R counts cannot be reconciled with design without revisiting the snRNA inference. Framed as the "snRNA call-inference issue" category — a residual of snRNA-only inference at HH17/16, where median per-genotype PITX2c+ fraction = 0% (noise floor). The flag propagates to **all genotypes in a failing block** (conservative single-modality rollup): with one modality a reader cannot tell whether a block fails for a row-specific or block-wide reason, so every block-mate inherits the flag. Resolved counterpart = **fig03 panel E**, which uses a per-genotype reconciliation rule and unflags rows whose own sides match a design candidate even when block-mates remain weak (see `docs/chromatin_canonical_hh1716.md` §3c).
  - **Orange** — design-intrinsic ambiguity with no pc_fail (cardinality 0, or asymmetric multi-candidate without an interchangeable peer): the (library × side) signature is consistent with ≥2 design embryos and no second modality shares a different coordinate that could break the tie. Persists in panel D and in fig03 panel E.
  - **Asterisk (no tint)** — symmetric/interchangeable peer group: ≥2 genotypes in the same library-pair share an identical candidate set; the within-set assignment is arbitrary but lossless (round-robin gives each peer a distinct representative). *(2026-08-21 fix: an **unresolved** multi-candidate row — cardinality > 1 with no lossless peer group, all conflict-flagged — now displays its full `|`-joined candidate set, e.g. `16-9|17-11|17-12`, instead of a fake-precise first pick; the pre-fix `cand_sorted[0]` default had fabricated duplicate representatives after the seeded d2l flipped two genotypes' sides and broke the candidate split.)*
- **As rendered (2026-08 seeded chain):** **12 red rows across 4 HH17/16 blocks** (17-Md-13/g20; g46/g52/g45/g44; g47; g17/g15/g19/g22/g23/g24); 0 orange rows; **37 peer-asterisks**. Footers: **12 of 15 blocks reconcile; 3 fail** — {GEX07, GEX08} (inferred 1L/5R vs design 3L/3R), {GEX04, GEX0S-duck} (GEX04 side 1L/3R vs design 2L/2R; GEX0S-duck 3L/1R vs 2L/2R), and the {GEX04} HH16-unpaired singleton. **donor8 (g47) carries no side call on the current d2l** (votes tied, `firmness = weak`, `inferred_side = ?`, candidates 16-4/16-5; the earlier "firm-resolved to 16-4 (L) by the singleton rule" applied to the pre-seeded d2l and is retired). The snRNA-only HH17/16 failures remain the "snRNA call-inference issue" category; the chromatin-arbitrated counterpart (fig03 panel E, per-genotype reconciliation on d2n sides) is what resolves them.

### Panel D (dotplot atlas; was panel E)
- Each lineage assignment is corroborated by ≥2 markers so row labels are not single-marker artifacts (e.g. NKX6-1 + IRX3 = dorsal; FOXF1 + FOXF2 + GATA4 + ZFPM2 = splanchnic).
- The 17-gene signaling set was chosen so each gene surfaces as a top differential signal in at least one stage's stage-restricted subclustering.
- Dot encoding: lineage/signaling/lncRNA/PITX2 columns — size = fraction expressing (clipped at 50%), color = standard-scaled mean CP10K per column. PITX2 isoform fractions max-normalised per column. Cell-cycle columns — size and color both encode the within-column phase fraction (max-normalised per column, same Greys colormap).
- The per-(stage × population × side) cell-cycle block replaces a coarser-granularity proliferation control (an earlier draft pseudobulked L vs R G2M across the whole LPM mesenchyme group); the finer block lets any per-population proliferation asymmetry surface directly.
- HH25 rows (current seeded SSOT): Dorsal sub-epi., Ventral spl., Syringeal (rendered "Syringeal chondro."), Venous-pole spl. (1,244 L / 1,068 R) and Skeletogenic LPM (the actual low-n row, 715 nuclei). The BARX1+, Mesothelium and Chondrogenic rows are absent at HH25 — Chondrogenic was folded into Syringeal, and cl8 / cl9 carry Ventral spl. / Venous-pole spl.
- The Venous-pole spl. row (GATA4+/ZFPM2+/TBX20+/TBX4-low/WT1-low) is the non-myocardial splanchnic envelope of the venous pole / proepicardium (van Wijk et al. 2009), captured by foregut-dissection-edge sampling and excluded from the laterality inference pool as off-axis tissue introgression. Mesothelium (WT1+/TCF21+) is the visceral coelomic surface lining — on-axis at the syrinx cross-section but bilateral by anatomy.
