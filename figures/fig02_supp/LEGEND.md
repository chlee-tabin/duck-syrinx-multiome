# Figure S9: Sampling balance, left/right inference, the left/right-resolved LPM subcluster atlas, and HH17/16 subcluster composition

**Supplementary to Figure 4; the underlying four-metric left/right inference is provided in full as Table S4.**

**(A)** Per-genotype paired-library nucleus counts, colored by inferred stage; bars are labeled by library (06, PG3, PG4A/4B, 03-1A/1B, 03-2, 04, 0S-duck, 05, 07, 08), and left/right is inferred in Table S4, not asserted here.

**(B)** Cell-type composition per genotype × library (stacked proportions; genotype axis as in A): members of a library pair sample the same embryo, with differences dominated by stage rather than left/right sampling. F examines the finer HH17/16 subclusters.

**(C)** Per-subcluster PITX2 laterality magnitude on the three Table S4 metrics (PITX2+ fraction, PITX2c+ fraction, mean PITX2 expression): dot = one paired genotype's within-pair fold, tick = median, dashed line = bilateral (1×); background bands = pool membership (dark = inference pool, medium = bilateral control, light = excluded). Inference-pool subclusters sit above 1× at HH13, HH21 and HH25; excluded subclusters are excluded for lineage reasons, not flatness (direction: Table S4).

**(D)** Left/right-resolved subcluster atlas: one dotplot row per stage × population × side (HH13/HH21/HH25 split by the Table S4 sides; HH17/16 collapsed, where the inference is weaker); column blocks = lineage markers, signaling-pathway genes, the PLAYRR-candidate lncRNA, PITX2 isoform fractions (as in Figure 4B), and cell-cycle phase fractions; dot size = fraction expressing, color = scaled mean expression, nucleus counts in parentheses. Dorsal sub-epithelial rows at HH21/HH25 show elevated PLAYRR* relative to the ventral splanchnic and syringeal rows, consistent with its PITX2-antagonistic biology (Welsh et al. 2015).

**(E)** Duck-chick synteny at the PITX2/PLAYRR locus: LASTZ alignment ribbons (Harris 2007) connect duck NC_051775.1 (3.40-4.20 Mb) to the orthologous chick region (greyscale = percent identity); duck LOC106016802 maps to the position of the chick e926/PLAYRR TSS, ~390 kb upstream of PITX2 — the chick PITX2 ↔ PLAYRR arrangement — supporting it as the duck positional ortholog of the asymmetric PITX2-antagonizing lncRNA.

**(F)** In most embryos the HH17/16 right bias of AHR and CHRM2 persists within subclusters; a variable right-half excess of Cardiopharyngeal LPM, in which both genes are enriched, adds to it. Left: Cardiopharyngeal LPM share of ventral inference-pool nuclei (remainder BARX1+) in each half of the 13 paired embryos with ≥ 20 pool nuclei per half (blue circle, left; red square, right; line, one embryo; tick, median; R > L, embryos with a higher right share; p, two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test). Right: right-minus-left pooled expression of AHR and CHRM2 in each embryo (counts per million, CPM; positive, higher on the right), split into a composition part (dark; from the halves' different subcluster shares of transcripts) and a within-subcluster part (light; see Methods); line, one embryo; tick, mean over embryos; k/13 > 0, embryos in which that part is above zero. AHR and CHRM2 are about 7- and 13-fold higher in Cardiopharyngeal LPM than in BARX1+ nuclei (median per embryo; higher in all 13 embryos), so a larger right-half share raises their pooled level. Per-stage fold changes are in Table S6 (left/right, so right-biased genes are negative there).
