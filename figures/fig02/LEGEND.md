# Figure 4: Single-nucleus multiome assay reveals PITX2c-specific left bias in prospective ventral syrinx mesenchyme

**PITX2c laterality in the ventral splanchnic and syringeal populations arises in two waves — HH13, then HH21–HH25 — with PITX2 RNA no longer reliably resolving the two halves at the intervening HH17/16, the trough stage resolved by chromatin in Figure 5; the dorsal compartment expresses PITX2 bilaterally from the shared exons.**

Panels A–D of this composite are manuscript Figure 4B–E. Manuscript Figure 4A is the global snRNA-seq atlas UMAP (176,933 nuclei; 12 libraries, 52 embryos), colored by cell type (16 types); the LPM and splanchnic mesenchyme lineages are the populations subclustered below.

**(A)** Structural-marker dotplot of the stage-restricted mesenchyme subclusters (nucleus counts in parentheses), markers grouped by the identity they mark (NKX6-1 dorsal; FOXF1/GATA4/ZFPM2 splanchnic; TBX5/TBX4 airway; BARX1 tracheo-esophageal septum; ISL1/HAND2 LPM/cardiopharyngeal; PRRX1 skeletogenic), followed by per-cell PITX2 isoform fractions (total, PITX2a/b, PITX2c); dot size = fraction of nuclei expressing, color = mean expression. "Syringeal chondro." is the syringeal chondrocyte precursor: IRX1-expressing at HH21, acquiring the cartilage-commitment program (RUNX2, WNT5A, DPP10, PTCHD4) by HH25 (Methods); at HH13–HH17/16 PRRX1 also marks a distinct PRRX1+/FOXF1-low mesenchyme without skeletal transcription factors.

**(B)** Cross-section schematic of the HH21 syrinx region locating the six resolved dorsal-ventral zones.

**(C)** PITX2 in the two halves of each embryo, plotted against each other. Top, fraction of PITX2+ nuclei; bottom, mean PITX2 expression. Facets show the left/right inference pool (LPM mesenchyme at HH13, ventral splanchnic pool from HH17/16) and the dorsal sub-epithelial control. Each point is one embryo, and the axes are its two library halves (Library A, Library B). The plot does not say which half is left (Table S4 does), so distance from the y = x line measures asymmetry in either direction. HH13 and HH25 embryos, the two waves of PITX2, sit well off the line, and HH21 embryos less so. HH17/16 embryos (red outline) vary. The labelled ones sit closer to the line than any HH13, HH21 or HH25 embryo on both measures. g36 and g37 are also labelled in the dorsal facets (g41 has too few dorsal nuclei to plot). From HH17/16 on, dorsal points as a group sit closer to the line than ventral points, relative to each facet's axis range. Two HH17 embryos (17-5, 17-6), whose two libraries hold the same left halves (Figure S7A), are not plotted.

**(D)** PITX2-locus snRNA pileup by stage and population, filtered to the PITX2 sense strand (the two annotated antisense ncRNAs excluded); ZJU1.0 gene model with the three isoform structures below. Dorsal signal concentrates at the shared exons and 3′UTR (total PITX2), whereas the left-biased ventral signal is PITX2c-specific.
