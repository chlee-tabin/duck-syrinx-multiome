# Figure S12: Chick syrinx multiome control

**A genotype-demultiplexed chick syrinx multiome reproduces the duck cell-type atlas (Figure S7), but its LPM PITX2 half-vs-half divergence tracks a library-level detection floor rather than laterality — the expected negative contrast with the duck's clean left-bias (Figure 4).** Colours follow the duck convention (shared lineage palette; Left = blue, Right = red; Male = blue, Female = red; GFP⁺ = green in demultiplexing panels).

**(A)** Design: 24 embryos across 3 stages (HH13, HH15-16, HH25), each bisected into Left + Right halves, demultiplexed in two groups. Group A (12 WT embryos): complementary halves split across two libraries, so per-embryo L/R orientation is unresolved (the halves are genetically identical). Group B (6 GFP⁺ + 6 WT embryos): a designed, verified lane→side map — the non-circular L/R control. GFP is a whole-animal demultiplexing label (green diamond = GFP⁺, open diamond = WT), treated identically to wild-type in every biological readout.

**(B)** Whole-embryo cell-type atlas UMAP (Groups A + B; 37,362 nuclei), Harmony-integrated on library; clusters annotated by argmax of the duck canonical lineage markers (avian orthologs). The chick recovers 11 of the 16 duck lineages at this depth, plus a "Mesenchyme (early)" stage pool — early/broad mesenchyme versus the HH25 TBX5/TBX4⁺ LPM mesenchyme, mirroring the duck's stage split, not a lineage; five minor duck types fold into their parent lineages (panel D), and sclerotome (PAX1⁺, chondrogenic) is surfaced inside the early pool, as in the duck.

**(C)** Developmental staging: LPM-lineage pseudobulk PCA per cohort (analog of Figure S7d), run separately per group because of the Group A/B batch effect. PC1 is the dominant developmental-stage axis and PC2 largely tracks sex (not perfectly axis-aligned). Colour = sex; shape = group/line; points labelled by donor; dashed convex hulls with a single-hue blue ramp = developmental order. All 24 embryos receive a concrete stage: Group A stages are fixed by an orthogonal physical anchor (a library that captured only the design-known LEFT halves of the HH15-16 + HH25 embryos) and independently confirmed by whole-embryo cell-type composition; Group B HH25 comes from the resolved demultiplexing, and its early embryos split into HH13 vs HH15-16 on PC1, constrained to the design counts.

**(D)** Marker dot plot: the Figure S7C marker panel minus KIF26B and NFIA, which are not annotated in chick (35 of 37 genes; dot size = fraction expressing, shade = per-gene standard-scaled mean expression; gene-group brackets above), with chick LEFTY2 in the duck LEFTY1 slot (the chick annotation’s ortholog of the duck gene). The folded-in minor lineages are visible as marker fold-ins: FOXF1/GATA4/ZFPM2 in the LPM mesenchyme row, FOXI2/TFAP2E in Epithelial, ARX in Dorsal neural prog.; PITX2 marks the LPM/mesenchyme rows.

**(E)** PITX2-locus snRNA pileup (Group B, stage × population), as in Figure 4e: sense-strand per-cell coverage over the chick PITX2 locus with the 3-isoform gene model, polyA internal-priming markers, and the PITX2c TSS (dashed red) — sense signal 5′ of the TSS can only be a/b-isoform transcription.

**(F)** Orientation-free PITX2 half-vs-half divergence by spatial domain (analog of Figure 4D): per embryo × domain, PITX2 in one half versus the other, distance from the y = x diagonal measuring asymmetry regardless of which half is which (half assignment arbitrary; every embryo contributes; Group A open, Group B filled). Facets = three domains (LPM mes. HH13 / Ventral splanchnic pool / Dorsal sub-epi. control); rows = PITX2⁺ fraction and mean CP10K. The LPM domains do scatter off the diagonal, but the divergence tracks the between-lane detection floor: the deeper library has the higher PITX2⁺ fraction in 14 of 16 stage × population comparisons, and no stage's divergence survives a lane-offset correction. Design-orientable donor labels are coloured by design concordance (black = concordant, red = inconsistent).

**(G)** Oriented cross-species PITX2 isoform × modality L/R contrast — the L/R-resolved supplement to Figure S11B: the same four readouts (ATAC ΔFRiP at the PITX2c/PITX2a/PITX2b promoters; total PITX2 RNA Δcp10k), signed L−R (positive = Left), across HH13/HH17-16/HH21/HH25 in the ventral splanchnic (syrinx-precursor) pool. Duck (black) carries an assigned L/R per half; chick, whose orientation is design-ambiguous, is shown as its design-legal best/worst envelope over the legal lane→side maps. Point area ∝ min-half nuclei; off-scale points become edge ▲/▼ glyphs with true values. Duck PITX2c is Left-biased; chick PITX2c is null even in its best case; PITX2a/b show no consistent L/R bias in either species (the within-isoform control).
