# Figure S16: No left excess of cycling cells in the HH25 syrinx-precursor pool

Left = blue circle, Right = red square throughout.

**(A)** Embryo-paired cycling-nucleus fraction (percentage of pool nuclei not in G1, composition-standardised) in left vs right halves of the HH25 syrinx-precursor pool (the ventral splanchnic and syringeal populations of Figure 4), one line per embryo; n = 8 embryos (16 half-embryo pseudobulks). Right subpanel: the per-embryo left-minus-right differences (points) with the Hodges-Lehmann estimate and exact distribution-free 95% confidence interval (black diamond and whiskers) and the zero reference line: Hodges-Lehmann = -2.9 percentage points, 95% CI [-5.3, -0.5], two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 0.039, 1 of 8 embryos left-higher. The dashed line marks the exclusion benchmark delta = +13.7 percentage points, the left cycling excess observed at E9; the interval upper bound lies below it, so a left excess of the size later observed at E9 is excluded at HH25.

**(B)** Composition control (specified in the analysis plan): the syringeal-precursor share of the pool per side, per embryo. The paired difference is null (Hodges-Lehmann -3.1 percentage points, p = 0.31), so the primary result is not an artifact of population-composition shifts between sides.

**(C)** Mitogen-program and ESR1 left-right read-out across development: per-gene left-vs-right log2 fold change (embryo-paired DESeq2; error bars = standard error; vertical line = no change) in the multiome syrinx-precursor pool at HH21 and HH25 (n = 8 paired embryos each; Table S6) and in the syrinx populations (syringeal fibroblast, chondrocyte and smooth muscle) of the E9/E11 scRNA-seq dataset, split by sex (embryos with both sides passing a 10-cell floor — E9: 4 male / 3 female; E11: 4 / 4). Filled circles are significant at adjusted P < 0.05 (Benjamini-Hochberg across genes passing DESeq2 independent filtering), open circles are not. The asymmetric mitogen field assembles between HH21 and HH25 — at HH25, BMP7 (adjusted P = 1.1 × 10⁻⁴), SFRP2 (1.1 × 10⁻⁸), SHISA2 (0.018) and BMP4 (3.7 × 10⁻⁵) are left-biased, and at HH21 only SFRP2 (0.030) among the genes shown — and precedes any proliferative asymmetry (MKI67 and TOP2A not significant at any stage or sex). ESR1 is left-shifted at HH25 (log2FC = +0.34) without reaching genome-wide significance at either multiome stage; its left bias is established in the growth phase (E9 males log2FC = +0.62, adjusted P = 6.5 × 10⁻⁹; E11 males +0.54, P = 1.8 × 10⁻⁴; E11 females +0.53, P = 0.042; not yet significant in E9 females at n = 3). The screen hits persist into the growth phase in a sex-patterned way: SFRP2 stays left-biased at E9 in both sexes (males P = 0.0496, females 9.8 × 10⁻⁷) and at E11 in females (3.9 × 10⁻³), SHISA2 in E9 males (3.3 × 10⁻⁸).
