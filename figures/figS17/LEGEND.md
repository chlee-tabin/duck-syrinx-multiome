# Figure S17: Left-biased ESR1 detection in the HH25 syrinx-precursor pool

**ESR1 detection is left-biased in the HH25 syrinx-precursor pool, before gonadal estrogen is available in either sex; the bias survives a per-cell sequencing-depth adjustment and is absent from the bilateral dorsal control.** Left = blue circle, Right = red square throughout. Panels A and B are the same paired plot on two pools; the pool is named on each y axis.

**(A)** Embryo-paired ESR1-positive detection fraction (percentage of pool nuclei with detected ESR1, composition-standardised) in left vs right halves of the HH25 syrinx-precursor pool (the ventral splanchnic and syringeal populations), one line per embryo; n = 8 embryos (16 half-embryo pseudobulks). Right, the per-embryo left-minus-right differences (points) with the Hodges-Lehmann estimate and exact distribution-free 95% confidence interval (black diamond and whiskers), and the zero reference line: Hodges-Lehmann = +9.6 percentage points left, 95% CI [+1.1, +18.4], one-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 0.0117 (direction pre-specified from the independently dissected E9/E11 dataset), 7 of 8 embryos left-higher.

**(B)** Bilateral control: the same estimator in the dorsal sub-epithelial population is null (Hodges-Lehmann -0.3 percentage points, p = 1.00; n = 6 embryos, the pool-size cohort rule drops 2).

Depth control (panel not shown): the detection bias does not track per-nucleus sequencing depth — 7 of 8 embryos are left-higher, including three of the four embryos whose right half was sequenced deeper, and a per-nucleus binomial mixed model with per-embryo random slopes for side and a log-depth offset confirms the left bias at +0.34 log-odds, p = 0.0025.
