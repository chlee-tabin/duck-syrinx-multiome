# Figure S15 (page 3 of 3): BMP ligands localize to the syrinx precursor, with left-biased BMP7

*Manuscript Figure S15, page 3 of 3, carrying panels **F–I**. Panel letters run continuously across the three pages: **A–D** on page 1 (WNT screen), **E** on page 2 (BMP- versus TGF-β-arm SMADs), **F–I** here.*

**The BMP-ligand screen of the E6/E9/E11 duck scRNA-seq, resolved into dorsal–ventral mesenchymal subpopulations rather than pooled mesoderm and tested for left/right asymmetry per cell, localizes the active ligands BMP4/BMP5 to the ventral splanchnic syrinx precursor and, with per-embryo left/right pairing, surfaces a left-biased BMP7 — while the receptors that read those ligands are present in every subpopulation, so it is the ligand field and not receptor availability that patterns the response.** Violins are coloured by mesenchymal subpopulation (canonical palette, as in Figure S14); Left = blue circle, Right = red square.

**(F)** BMP ligands by mesenchymal subpopulation across developmental stage. Per-gene violins of log-normalized expression for the BMP-ligand family (BMP1–BMP15; 9 genes), subpopulations on the x-axis and stage (E06/E09/E11) as nested columns; black point = median. The family is split into two side-by-side blocks — BMP1–BMP5 on the left, BMP6–BMP15 on the right — each with its own stage columns and subpopulation axis. All panel genes are shown so the specificity is visible: BMP4 and BMP5 are not uniformly expressed across mesoderm but concentrate in the Ventral splanchnic population at E06 — the syrinx precursor. Of the remaining ligands, BMP6 shows a modest E06 ventral-splanchnic signal and BMP2 low broad expression; BMP1/BMP3/BMP7/BMP8A/BMP15 are low-to-absent at the transcript level (BMP7's contribution is the per-embryo left bias in panel G, not abundance here).

**(G)** Per-cell expression of the BMP-pathway hits, left versus right, paired by embryo. For BMP4, BMP5, BMP7 and PITX2 (positive laterality reference), each point is one embryo's mean per-cell expression (CP10K) in the syrinx-fated mesenchyme on one half; grey lines pair the halves of the same embryo; bar = median; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank. BMP7 is left-biased at E09 (paired p = 0.022, n = 7) alongside the PITX2 control (p = 0.022), whereas BMP4 (p = 0.076/0.53 at E09/E11) and BMP5 (p = 0.93/0.14) are symmetric across halves; by E11 BMP7 is symmetric too (p = 0.94). The comparison is of per-cell expression level, not cell number. Complementary gene-level tests of the growth-phase left-right contrasts (embryo-paired DESeq2 split by sex, genome-wide correction) appear in Fig. S16C.

**(H)** BMP receptor competence across mesenchymal subpopulations, with BMP4 as the ligand-source contrast. Dot size = percentage of cells with detectable transcript; greyscale fill = mean expression (CP10K) on one shared scale; genes on the x-axis (BMP4 to the right of the dashed rule) and subpopulations on the y-axis, grouped into stage blocks (E06/E09/E11) so each stage carries only the subpopulations present at it; cells pooled across left and right halves. The two receptors that every BMP signal requires are broadly available: BMPR1A is detected in 40–75% of cells and BMPR2 in 62–86%, in every subpopulation at every stage and at comparable mean levels — no subpopulation lacks the machinery to receive a BMP signal. BMPR1B is the one receptor that narrows: broadly present at E06 (37–66% of cells), it concentrates onto the Syringeal chondrocyte lineage from E09 (mean 4.35 CP10K) to E11 (8.05 CP10K, 73% of cells), against 1.1–1.8 in Syringeal fibroblast and 0.2–0.6 in Syringeal smooth muscle. Right of the rule, BMP4 is not co-located with that high-BMPR1B responder — at E09 it is highest in Syringeal fibroblast (33.9% of cells) and lowest in the chondrocyte (14.5%), a paracrine rather than autocrine arrangement. Because reception is broad, the subpopulation that responds is not selected by receptor availability; the single restricted receptor marks the chondrogenic lineage that differentiates late.
