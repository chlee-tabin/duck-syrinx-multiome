# Figure S15 (page 1 of 3): WNT pathway in the syrinx-forming mesenchyme, resolved by subpopulation

*Manuscript Figure S15, page 1 of 3. Panel letters run continuously across the three pages: **A–D** here, **E** on page 2 (BMP- versus TGF-β-arm SMADs), **F–I** on page 3 (BMP ligands and receptors).*

**The WNT-ligand and pathway-modulator screen of the E6/E9/E11 duck scRNA-seq, resolved into dorsal–ventral mesenchymal subpopulations rather than pooled mesoderm and tested for left/right asymmetry per cell, localizes each WNT to its cellular source and recovers a gene-specific laterality axis.** Violins are coloured by mesenchymal subpopulation (canonical palette, as in Figure S14); Left = blue circle, Right = red square.

**(A)** WNT ligands and pathway modulators by mesenchymal subpopulation across developmental stage. Per-gene violins of log-normalized expression, with subpopulations (Ventral splanchnic, Dorsal sub-epithelial, Syringeal chondrocyte, Syringeal fibroblast/smooth muscle, Skeletogenic LPM, Mesothelium) on the x-axis and stage (E06/E09/E11) as nested columns; black point = median. Only WNT genes expressed in at least one subpopulation are shown. WNT2B marks the dorsal sub-epithelial population, WNT5A is broadest at E06, and WNT5B/WNT11 rise at E09/E11; SFRP2 is broadly expressed and SHISA2 concentrates in the Syringeal chondrocyte.

**(B)** Per-cell expression of the WNT-pathway hits, left versus right, paired by embryo. For each screen hit (WNT5A, SFRP2, SHISA2; the ISH gene is the paralog WNT5B, panel C) and PITX2 as a positive laterality reference, each point is one embryo's mean per-cell expression (CP10K) in the syrinx-fated mesenchyme on one half; grey lines pair the two halves of the same embryo; bar = median; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank. PITX2 is left-biased at E09 (the expected positive control; paired p = 0.022, n = 7) and SFRP2 is left-biased at E11 (p = 0.014, n = 8; E09 p = 0.052, n = 7); the other WNT hits are symmetric (WNT5A p = 0.35/0.36, SHISA2 p = 0.15/0.44 at E09/E11; PITX2 E11 p = 1), so the laterality is gene-specific. The comparison is of per-cell expression level, not cell number.

**(C)** In situ hybridization of the screen hits (WNT5B, SFRP2, SHISA2, BMP7, ESR1; male, E9–E13), reproduced from the manuscript's candidate in situ figure (Figure S18) for direct comparison with panel D. Right (R) and left (L) as labelled.

**(D)** The Figure S18 in situ candidates read onto mesenchymal subpopulations. Mean expression (shade) and fraction of cells expressing (dot size) for each gene (columns) in each mesenchymal subpopulation (rows), with stage as stacked row blocks (E06/E09/E11; only the subpopulations present at a stage are shown). The differing in situ domains in panel C correspond to distinct subpopulations: SHISA2 to the Syringeal chondrocyte (its segmented, cartilage-condensation pattern), SFRP2 to the broad Syringeal fibroblast, and BMP4/BMP5 to the Ventral splanchnic (syrinx precursor), while BMP7 is restricted and low.
