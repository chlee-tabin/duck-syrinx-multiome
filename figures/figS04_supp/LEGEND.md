# Chick multiome QC (supports Figure S12): Chick snRNA-seq, snATAC-seq, and genotype demultiplexing QC

**A QC companion to Figure S12 for the chick syrinx cross-species control, mirroring the duck Figures S8 (library QC + demultiplexing validation), S9 (sampling balance), and S10 (snATAC QC), restricted to what the chick control needs: per-library snRNA quality metrics, orthogonal genotype-concordance validation of the demultiplexing, W/Z sex calls, per-embryo left/right half sampling, and per-library snATAC QC. snRNA library names use the uniform GEX convention (Group A = GEX01/GEX02, the GEX modality of the ARC multiome lanes; Group B = GEX09/GEX10); GEX0S-chick, the Left-only symmetry-breaking lane, carries generally fewer nuclei and is omitted from the snRNA panels for clarity (it appears in the panel C concordance projection and, as its ATAC modality ARC0S-chick, in the panel E snATAC QC). Sex Male = blue / Female = red; Left = blue / Right = red; everything else grayscale.**

**(A)** snRNA-seq per-library QC. Per-lane metrics for the four snRNA libraries: nucleus count, median UMI/nucleus, median genes/nucleus, mean mitochondrial %, and vireo doublet rate.

**(B)** Resolved-12 W/Z sex violins (Group B). Per-donor distributions of % W-gene UMIs (top; ZW females elevated, ZZ males ≈ 0) and % Z-gene UMIs (bottom; ZZ males ≈ 2× ZW females), after the resolved-12 relabel. Color = sex (Male blue / Female red).
**(C)** Group A cross-lane genotype concordance. Embryo × embryo pseudobulk allele-frequency correlation grids across the three Group A lanes; a bright same-embryo diagonal over a dim off-diagonal indicates clean, genotypically-distinct embryos. GEX01 × GEX02 is the cross-lane concordance check (a within-lane merge of two embryos would show as a low same-embryo r); the GEX0S-chick × GEX01 / GEX0S-chick × GEX02 grids are a projection-assignment QC — GEX0S-chick (the Left-only HH15-16/HH25 anchor) was assigned by matching these same genotypes, so its same-embryo diagonal is expected by construction. The independent signal there is the presence pattern: exactly the 8 HH15-16 + HH25 left-half embryos light up while the 4 HH13 embryos carry no GEX0S-chick row, matching the Left-only design (a built-in negative control).

**(D)** Per-embryo L/R half sampling by stage (the analog of the duck sampling balance, Figure S9A). The chick is bisected into Left + Right halves; each embryo's two physical halves are sequenced as the two libraries/lanes. Per embryo, the two half nucleus counts are drawn as paired bars, grouped by firm stage (HH13 / HH15-16 / HH25; 4 embryos per stage per group). Group B's lane→side map is designed, so its design-orientable embryos (uniform or singleton design class) are colored Left (blue) / Right (red) — including donor8 (HH25-GFP⁺, GEX09 = L / GEX10 = R); the rest are grey "half" bars where the per-embryo side is genuinely not resolvable — all of Group A (GEX01/GEX02 are complementary halves of genetically identical sides) and the non-uniform Group B classes. donor8's Right/GEX10 half is ≈97% under-captured (32 nuclei, the visibly tiny red bar), so donor8 is the one embryo excluded from the half-vs-half laterality in Figure S12F on data-quality grounds, even though its design side is known.

**(E)** Per-library snATAC-seq QC (the chick analog of Figure S10 panel A), over the 5 chick ARC multiome libraries (sub-panels E1–E7). Dodged bars/violins compare the initial nuclei count (dark) with the QC-filtered nuclei count (light; TSS enrichment ≥ 2 and ≥ 1000 fragments per nucleus): E1 nuclei per library; E2 ATAC raw reads per nucleus; E3 duplicate rate; E4 high-quality fragments per nucleus; E5 TSS-fragment fraction; E6 fraction of reads in peaks; E7 AMULET doublet rate. Per-nucleus median TSS enrichment is tightly clustered (≈5.2–5.7), well clear of the gate and numerically comparable to the duck; QC retention is ~73–80% of called nuclei for the four v2.1.0 libraries (the single v2.0.0 library, ARC0S-chick, exceeds it because its more conservative cell-calling under-counts relative to the fragment gate); AMULET doublet rates span 3.5–12.8% (median 5.5%), comparable to the duck cohort.
